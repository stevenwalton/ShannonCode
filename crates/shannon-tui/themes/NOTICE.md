# The shipped themes, and where their colours come from

Each file here is a theme `tui.theme` can name. Its colours are the
canonical palette of the theme it is named for — the sixteen terminal
colours and the ground its authors publish — taken from the upstream source
below, not from a terminal's copy (ghostty's bundled copies drift from
upstream in several of these). How Shannon's roles use them (which colour a
decision's fill wears, what a light theme's text is) is ours.

| tokyonight-{night,storm,moon,day} | github.com/folke/tokyonight.nvim, `extras/` | Apache-2.0 (the original VS Code theme by enkia is MIT) |
